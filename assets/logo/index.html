Infinity-hyprland: logo concepts

Five directions, each as a square icon (256×256, rounded plate) and a horizontal logo with the name. The text is converted to outlines, so the files need no fonts. Pick one and say what to change.

01 Lemniscate with a window

A bold infinity sign with a window at the crossing: the canvas and the windows on it. The most direct reading.

02 Viewport

03 Linked windows

Two window frames locked together into an infinity shape: windows that are one connected space.
